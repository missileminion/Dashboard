Weight and pressure

Every block carries weight: exams, deadlines and labs count for more than a lecture, back-to-back blocks add strain, and long days add up. Heavy blocks look heavier, and each day's header glows with how tight it is, so the week reads at a glance without reading titles.
How weight is worked out: exam, midterm, final, quiz, due, deadline count 3×; lab, project, presentation, interview 1.6×; run, gym, workout 1.2×; anything else 1×. Each block's weight is its length in hours times that factor. A gap under 15 minutes between blocks adds 0.4. The day's pressure is the sum, shown out of 10 (a score of 10 is roughly a 9-hour heavy day).
